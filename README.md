## 🚗 About Team Avinya

Team Avinya is the official All-Terrain Vehicle (ATV) design and manufacturing team under the Open Throttle Racing Club (OTR) at Ajeenkya DY Patil University.

The team participates in the prestigious eBAJA SAEINDIA competition, focusing on innovation, vehicle design, electrical systems, powertrain integration, and real-world engineering challenges.
