Meet Team Avinya

Where innovation meets adrenaline — building tomorrow's electric ATVs today.

Who We Are

→ → →

Team Avinya is the heartbeat of the Open Throttle Racing Club at ADYPU. We're a student-led team driven by a passion for electric mobility, sustainable engineering, and real-world innovation. Under the O.T.R Club, we collaborate across disciplines — mechanical, electrical, and computer science — to design, build, and race high-performance electric ATVs.

Our team proudly represents the university at national platforms like SAEINDIA eBAJA and other ATV competitions, showcasing a blend of creativity, technical skill, and teamwork. We believe in learning by doing and pushing the limits of what's possible.

Our Journey

  • 2023: Team Avinya founded by passionate students. Despite being new, our interdisciplinary collaboration allowed us to compete with established teams.
  • 2024: Built and tested our first prototype, overcoming technical challenges. Proved our ability to turn ideas into reality.
  • 2025: First eBAJA competition: cleared Weight Test, Mechanical and Electrical Technical Inspections, and Validation Test. Endured Rainstorm Test, earning recognition from industry experts.
  • 2025: Felicitated by Honorable Chairman for innovation, teamwork, and resilience. Our journey continues with excitement for the future.

Achievements

🏆 Weight Test

Successfully cleared the vehicle weight requirements, demonstrating our commitment to efficiency and smart engineering.

🔧 Technical Inspection

Passed both Mechanical & Electrical technical inspections, validating our design, safety, and attention to detail.

🌧️ Rainstorm Test

Completed the challenging rainstorm endurance test, proving our ATV's durability and our team's resilience under pressure.

Departments

Powertrain

Designing and optimizing the electric powertrain system. Our Powertrain team ensures maximum efficiency and performance, turning electrical energy into speed and reliability on the track.

Vehicle Dynamics

Suspension and steering systems for optimal performance. This team fine-tunes every aspect of handling, ensuring our ATV is agile, stable, and ready for any challenge.

Electrical

Battery management and electrical systems. The Electrical team powers our innovation, from safe battery integration to advanced telemetry and controls.

Braking

Advanced braking systems for safety and performance. Our Braking team ensures we can stop as confidently as we go, with robust, reliable systems.

Chassis & Manufacturing

Structural design and fabrication. This team brings our ideas to life, building a strong, lightweight chassis that forms the backbone of our ATV.

Static & Marketing

Documentation, outreach, and team management. The Static & Marketing team tells our story, manages our brand, and ensures our work is recognized and supported.
